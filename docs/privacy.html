Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Danzo ("the app", "we") operates the Danzo mobile app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Account: when you sign in with Apple we receive an Apple account identifier. During sign-up you provide a username and your date of birth, and you can optionally add a profile photo, a bio and your phone number.

Content: photos, videos, captions, comments, likes and the parties you create or join.

Location: your device location while the app is open, to show parties near you and to place a party you create. When you open the app, we also save your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km), only to let you know when people you follow start a party near you; it is never shown to other users. We never collect location in the background.

Contacts (optional): if you choose to sync your contacts, you are asked for consent first, and the phone numbers in your address book are then sent to our servers only to find which of your contacts already use Danzo. They are not saved, shown to other users or used for any other purpose.

Notifications (optional): if you turn on notifications, we store a device token so we can send them.

App opens: we record which days you open the app and your app version, linked to your account, to measure whether people come back. This record does not include your location or content, and it is deleted with your account.

How you found Danzo (optional): if you answer this question after signing up, we save your answer with your account to understand how people discover the app.

Invite codes (optional): if you enter an invite code, we save it with your account to know which invite brought you to Danzo and whether you later joined an event. The person who shared the code does not see who used it.

2. How we use your information

We use your information only to operate the app: showing parties near you on the map, sharing your content with the people allowed to see it, helping friends find each other, sending the notifications you turned on, keeping the community safe, and understanding how often people return so we can improve the app. We do not sell or rent your personal information. The only information shared for advertising is the ad measurement described in section 7.

3. Who can see your information

Your username, profile photo and bio are visible to other users. Photos and videos posted in a public party are visible to its attendees. Posts on your profile are visible only to followers you have accepted. Private parties are visible only to their host and guests with the invite code. Your phone number and date of birth are never shown on your profile.

4. Storage and security

Your information is stored by Supabase, our cloud infrastructure provider, on servers in the United States, encrypted in transit and at rest. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data.

5. Data retention

Photos and videos posted inside an event are deleted automatically shortly after the event ends. Posts on your profile stay until you delete them or delete your account. When you delete your account, all of your data is permanently deleted within 30 days. If a post was reported, we may keep its photo or video for up to 30 days for safety review, even if the post is deleted.

6. Your rights

You can access and edit your profile at any time, block other users, report inappropriate content, revoke location, contacts, camera or notification permissions in your device settings, and permanently delete your account and all of your data from the app settings.

7. Tracking and advertising

Danzo does not show ads inside the app and does not use tracking cookies. We do not ask to track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).

Meta ad measurement: we advertise Danzo on Instagram and use Meta's SDK to measure whether those ads lead to installs and sign-ups. The SDK sends Meta app events, such as app installs and opens, purchases and completed sign-ups, along with technical information such as your device, app version and IP address. Meta handles this information under its Privacy Policy.

8. Age requirement

Danzo is only for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that a minor has created an account, we delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time and will let you know about significant changes in the app. Continuing to use Danzo after changes means you accept the updated policy.
